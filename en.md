## In one sentence

Forgeheart is an offline game that keeps your progress on your phone. The only
data that leaves it is your device's advertising identifier, sent to Google to
display the ads that fund the game, and only if you have consented to it.

## Your consent, first

Before the very first ad, a screen asks whether Google may use your device's
advertising identifier. This screen is provided by Google ("User Messaging
Platform" tool): it lists the advertising partners involved and collects your
answer. It is the same on Android and on iPhone.

- **You can decline.** The game stays complete and free.
- **You can change your mind**, either way, at any time: Settings, then
  "Ad privacy".

**On Android**: without consent, no ad request is sent, so no ads are shown.

**On iPhone**: iOS adds its own permission, "App Tracking Transparency". After
an explanation screen from Google, a system window asks whether you allow the
app to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you
decline, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. You can change this
choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

## What is collected, and by whom

**A single piece of data: your device's advertising identifier**
("Advertising ID" on Android, IDFA on iPhone). It is a number assigned to your
device by the system, which you can reset or delete from your phone's settings
(see "Deletion").

- **Collected by**: the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) advertising kit, built into
  the app.
- **Shared with**: Google and its advertising partners, who use it to choose
  which ads are shown and for ad measurement and billing.
- **Received by the publisher**: nothing. Youkar sees neither this identifier
  nor any data about you. Only anonymous totals (number of ads shown, revenue)
  can be viewed in the AdMob dashboard.

Google's privacy policy applies to this processing:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## What the app does not do

- No account, no registration, no identification.
- No server, no syncing, no online backup.
- No analytics tool, no tracking of what you do in the game.
- No access to contacts, photos, location, or microphone.

## Permissions requested

### On Android

- `VIBRATE`: haptic feedback during forging gestures (the hammer's impact, the
  successful window). It gives access to no information and can be turned off in
  the game settings.
- `INTERNET`: needed to load ads and the consent screen. The game itself works
  without a network.
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`: reading the advertising
  identifier, requested by the Google Mobile Ads kit.
- `com.android.vending.BILLING`: added by the purchase library, it only allows
  communication with Google Play for the in-app purchase. It gives access to
  none of your information.

The advertising kit adds a few others, purely technical and with no access to
your information: network connection state (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`), Android's
Privacy Sandbox interfaces, and what is needed to finish a background upload
(`WAKE_LOCK`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`).

**What is not there, and will not be**: location, contacts, camera,
microphone, storage of your files. No permission of this type is declared. You
can check this yourself in the app's information on your phone.

### On iPhone

Forgeheart requests no access permission: no photos, no location, no
microphone, no contacts. The only permission requested is the tracking one
("App Tracking Transparency"), described above.

## What is saved, and where

Your progress (gold, forged pieces, rack, discovered recipes, settings) is
written **to your phone's private storage**, to which no other app has access.
Nothing leaves it.

The "Export save" function produces a text that **only you** copy and pass on,
if you wish. The app sends it nowhere.

## Deletion

Uninstalling the app erases all of your progress. No copy exists elsewhere: the
publisher has nothing to delete on their side, since they never received
anything.

For the advertising identifier:

- **Android**: in Settings > Google > Ads, you can reset it or delete it for
  all apps at once.
- **iPhone**: in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, you can refuse
  tracking, for Forgeheart or for all apps.

## In-app purchase

The game offers a single, optional, non-consumable in-app purchase, "Remove
ads" (€1.99 in France, local price elsewhere). The transaction is processed
**entirely by Google Play** (Android) or by **Apple's App Store** (iPhone). The
publisher receives no payment data, and no banking details pass through the app
or are visible to it.

The app keeps only an "ads removed" flag on your phone. If you reinstall the
game, the restore function queries the store to find your purchase.

After this purchase, the advertising kit is no longer started: no more ad
requests are sent.

For App Store purchases, Apple's privacy policy applies:
<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>. For Google Play, Google's, stated
above.

## Children

Forgeheart is not specifically aimed at children and is not designed for them.
It asks no one for any information: no name, no age, no address, no account. The
only data involved remains the advertising identifier described above, which
can be declined (consent screen, and tracking permission on iPhone) and deleted
from your phone's settings.
